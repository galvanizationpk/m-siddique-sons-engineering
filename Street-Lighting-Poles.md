## Introduction to Street Lighting Pole Manufacturing

Street lighting poles are essential infrastructure components used to support luminaires and provide illumination for roads, highways, streets, industrial facilities and commercial developments.

## Types of Street Lighting Poles

### 1. Octagonal Street Lighting Poles

Octagonal poles feature eight-sided tapered steel shafts.

They are widely used for road lighting and infrastructure applications because of their structural geometry and compatibility with fabricated lighting brackets.

Applications include:

- Urban roads.
- Highways.
- Industrial areas.
- Housing societies.
- Commercial developments.

### 2. Polygonal Lighting Poles

Polygonal poles are manufactured with multiple flat shaft surfaces.

Their dimensions, height, taper and wall thickness can be customized according to project engineering requirements.

### 3. Tubular Lighting Poles

Tubular poles use circular hollow steel sections or fabricated tubular shafts.

They are suitable for different lighting configurations and architectural requirements.

### 4. Single-Arm Lighting Poles

Single-arm poles support one lighting bracket and luminaire.

They are frequently used for standard road and street lighting.

### 5. Double-Arm Lighting Poles

Double-arm poles support two lighting brackets, typically arranged in opposite directions or according to the approved design.

These configurations can serve wider road arrangements.

### 6. High-Mast Lighting Poles

High-mast lighting systems are used where a large area requires illumination from a centralized elevated structure.

Applications include:

- Stadiums.
- Airports.
- Industrial yards.
- Ports.
- Large parking facilities.
- Highway interchanges.

### 7. Decorative Lighting Poles

Decorative poles are designed for parks, commercial developments, housing communities and architectural lighting projects.

### 8. CCTV and Integrated Lighting Poles

Specialized steel poles can incorporate CCTV camera brackets, lighting fixtures and cable routing arrangements.

7. Base plate fabrication.
8. Arm and bracket manufacturing.
9. Access door fabrication.
