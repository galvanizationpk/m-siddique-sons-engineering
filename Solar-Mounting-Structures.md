## Introduction to Solar Mounting Structures

Solar mounting structures are engineered support systems designed to securely install photovoltaic (PV) panels on rooftops, open land and other suitable locations.

These structures are an important component of solar power installations because they provide the required panel positioning, mechanical support and resistance to environmental loads.

M. Siddique Sons Engineering Concerns (Pvt.) Ltd. provides steel fabrication and engineering capabilities relevant to solar mounting structures and industrial steel infrastructure.

## What Are Solar Mounting Structures?

Solar mounting structures consist of fabricated steel or other suitable materials used to support photovoltaic panels.

A typical ground-mounted system may include:

- Main support columns.
- Horizontal beams.
- Purlins.
- Panel mounting rails.
- Bracing members.
- Connection plates.
- Base plates.
- Anchor bolts.
- Foundation connections.

The design depends on the panel dimensions, installation location, required tilt angle, environmental conditions and structural loading.

## Types of Solar Mounting Structures

### 1. Ground-Mounted Solar Structures

Ground-mounted solar structures are installed on prepared foundations or ground support systems.

They are suitable for:

- Solar farms.
- Industrial solar projects.
- Commercial solar installations.
- Open-land solar plants.
- Large-scale photovoltaic facilities.

### 2. Rooftop Solar Mounting Structures

Rooftop mounting systems support solar panels installed on industrial, commercial and residential roofs.

The design must consider the existing roof capacity, mounting method, waterproofing and applicable wind loads.

### 3. Fixed-Tilt Solar Structures

Fixed-tilt structures hold photovoltaic panels at a predetermined angle.

The tilt is selected according to project location, solar design requirements and structural considerations.

### 4. Customized Solar Steel Structures

Specialized fabricated structures can be designed according to:

- Panel arrangement.
- Site dimensions.
- Roof geometry.
- Foundation conditions.
- Required clearance.
- Project-specific installation requirements.

## Solar Structure Components

### Steel Columns

Columns transfer structural loads to foundations or supporting building elements.

### Beams and Purlins

These members support solar panel mounting arrangements and distribute loads.

### Bracing Systems

Bracing provides structural stability and resistance against lateral forces.

### Mounting Rails

Rails support photovoltaic panels and facilitate their mechanical attachment.

### Base Plates and Anchor Bolts

These components connect the structure to the foundation or roof support system.

## Engineering Considerations

Solar mounting structures must be designed according to project conditions.

Important factors include:

- Solar panel dimensions and weight.
- Panel orientation.
- Tilt angle.
- Wind loading.
- Dead loads.
- Snow loading where applicable.
- Corrosion exposure.
- Foundation design.
- Soil bearing capacity.
- Structural deflection.
- Roof capacity.
- Installation and maintenance access.

The final structural design should be verified by qualified engineers.
