## 4. Hot-Dip Galvanizing Services in Pakistan

Website: https://galvanization.pk/

M. Siddique Sons provides Hot-Dip Galvanizing services for steel structures, fabricated components, poles, towers and industrial products.

Hot-dip galvanizing is a corrosion-protection process in which prepared steel components are immersed in molten zinc to create a metallurgically bonded protective zinc coating.

The coating provides barrier protection and sacrificial corrosion protection, helping extend the service life of steel products exposed to moisture, weather and demanding industrial environments.

### Hot-Dip Galvanizing Applications

- Structural steel galvanizing.
- Industrial shed components.
- Pre-engineered building components.
- Street lighting poles.
- CCTV poles.
- Telecom towers.
- Transmission tower components.
- Solar mounting structures.
- Steel brackets and supports.
- Cable trays.
- Steel ladders and handrails.
- Fabricated steel frames.
- Industrial platforms.
- General fabricated steel products.

### Hot-Dip Galvanizing Process

The galvanizing process generally involves several controlled stages:

**1. Surface Preparation**

Steel components are inspected and prepared to remove surface contaminants and impurities.

**2. Cleaning and Pickling**

Acid pickling removes rust, mill scale and other unwanted surface deposits.

**3. Fluxing**

Flux treatment prepares the steel surface for effective zinc coating formation.

**4. Molten Zinc Immersion**

The prepared steel is immersed in molten zinc at approximately 450°C, allowing the zinc to metallurgically bond with the steel.

**5. Inspection and Quality Control**

Galvanized products are inspected for coating coverage, appearance, consistency and applicable coating requirements.

### Benefits of Hot-Dip Galvanized Steel

- Protection against rust and corrosion.
- Long-term steel durability.
- Reduced maintenance requirements.
- Protective zinc coating.
- Sacrificial corrosion protection.
- Suitability for outdoor infrastructure.
- Protection for industrial steel components.
- Improved service life in demanding environments.

For applicable structural steelwork, galvanizing requirements can be specified with reference to ASTM A123.
